You can request deletion of your Aastrosphere account and all associated data at any time. We currently handle deletion requests by email — here's exactly what happens.

How to request deletion

  1. Email aditya94727@gmail.com from the phone number or email linked to your Aastrosphere account (or tell us the phone number your account uses).
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my Aastrosphere account."
  3. We verify the request and delete your account within 7 days.
Email us to delete my account

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in record (Firebase Authentication)
  • Your chart data — name, date of birth, and any Circle entries you added
  • Your Ask Anything chat history
  • Your saved reports (if you're an astrologer account)

What we retain, and why

  • Purchase records — Google Play retains transaction history per its own policies, separate from Aastrosphere; we don't control this.
  • Basic financial/tax records of any completed transaction, kept only as long as legally required for accounting purposes, with all personal chart/chat data removed from them.

Everything else — your personal chart, chat history, and profile — is permanently deleted, not just deactivated.
