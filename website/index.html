Straightforward. Accurate. Consistent.

Vedic numerology is India's oldest predictive science — and one of its most precise. Aastrosphere is the first app to deliver it as a real, computed, personal reading, instead of a generic horoscope copy-pasted for everyone born that day.

Two systems — quick explainer
Western
The simpler system most global apps use (Co-Star, CHANI). Based on your name and birth date added together. Popular in the US/Europe.
Vedic
India's own, older system — also called Ank Jyotish. Reads deeper life-cycles from your birth date. This is what most Indian households actually consult, and it's what Aastrosphere is built entirely on.
