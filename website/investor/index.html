AstroSure.ai — how we're different.

The nearest thing to Aastrosphere in this market — still built on a fundamentally different engine underneath.

Under the hood: Per Inc42's founder interview (Aug 2025), Agastyaa is built directly on general-purpose models — GPT and Gemini — prompted with astrology texts. Aastrosphere runs the other way: a purpose-built 5-layer computed engine calculates the chart mathematically first, and a language model only writes up what's already computed. The prediction comes from the layer, not the model.
